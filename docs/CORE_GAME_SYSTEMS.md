# Core Game Systems

## 3D Movement and Flight
- Third-person controller with full 3D flight.
- Ground and air states allow seamless transition between running, jumping, gliding, and full-flight.
- Flight Control stat improves agility, turn speed, and precision during aerial combat.
- Environmental navigation includes city canyons, asteroid belts, orbital platforms, and alien wilderness.

## Combat System
- Combo-based melee and ranged attacks.
- Special abilities: shockwaves, gravity punches, sonic slams, and energy beams.
- Air combos use vertical momentum and aerial dash resets.
- Finishers trigger cinematic executions or crowd-clearing assaults.
- Enemy AI adapts to player strength, using evasion, flanking, and aerial pursuit.

## Progression and Power Level
- Stats: Strength, Speed, Durability, Combat Skill, Flight Control, Reputation.
- Power Level system unlocks abilities, planets, and story missions.
- Experience gained from missions, training, combat, rescues, and world events.
- Skill tree branches for Hero, Conqueror, and Hybrid builds.
- Reputation shifts based on mission outcomes, choices, and NPC feedback.

## Mission Types
### Earth Hero Missions
- Civilian rescues and emergency response.
- Crime prevention, villain takedowns, and base defenses.
- Anti-Viltrumite raids and space threat interceptions.
- Planetary liberation and cleanup after conquest.

### Viltrumite Missions
- Planet scouting and champion elimination.
- Resistance suppression, infrastructure destruction, and subjugation.
- Imperial training drills and large-scale invasion leadership.
- Rival Viltrumite clashes and betrayal-focused story beats.

## World Features
- Unique planets with their own environmental hazards, cultures, and defenders.
- Destructible environments including cities, battlefields, and alien structures.
- NPC hubs and social spaces for mission briefing, shopping, and story beats.
- Random events: invasions, rebellions, emergencies, and cosmic disturbances.
- Alignment-shifting moral decisions that alter available missions and dialogue.

## Story and Branching Narrative
- Two main story arcs, one for Earth heroes and one for Viltrumites.
- Branching nodes with alignment tests, betrayals, and redemption opportunities.
- Story choices affect ending, world state, and NPC relationships.
- Multiple endings based on alignment, power progression, and key decisions.

## Multiplayer / Coop Consideration (Optional)
- Potential for co-op missions by pairing a hero and a Viltrumite on neutral ground.
- Shared world events allow different players to impact planetary states.
- Not required for core single-player design, but the architecture supports it.
