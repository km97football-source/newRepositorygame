# ZoneWars: Open-World 3D Action RPG

## Vision
Create a fully 3D open-world action RPG inspired by *Invincible*, with two origin paths: a Viltrumite conqueror or an Earth hero. Players fly, fight, and explore across multiple planets with immersive 3D environments, destructible terrain, and cinematic aerial combat.

## Core Features
- 3D open-world exploration across Earth, Viltrum, and original/series-inspired worlds.
- Two origin paths:
  - **Viltrumite Conqueror**: Raised on Viltrum, trained for dominance, and sent to subjugate worlds.
  - **Earth Hero**: Raised on Earth, learns heroism, fights villains, and helps defend planets.
- Dynamic missions, side quests, and emergent world events.
- Third-person perspective with deep aerial and ground combat.

## Gameplay Systems
### 3D Movement
- Free-flight in 3D space with altitude, thrust, and maneuvering.
- Ground traversal with dashes, wall-runs, and short-range teleports.
- Seamless transitions between sky combat and surface combat.
- Environmental traversal through cityscapes, alien forests, and floating ruins.

### Combat
- Fast-paced combo system with ground and air chains.
- Special abilities and finishers that use 3D space.
- Aerial dogfights, ground brawls, and area-of-effect super moves.
- Destructible environments: buildings collapse, terrain breaks, and debris becomes cover.

### Progression
- Stats: Strength, Speed, Durability, Combat Skill, Flight Control, Reputation.
- Power level tiers unlock new abilities, planets, and story arcs.
- Skill tree with branches for hero, conqueror, and hybrid builds.
- Suit/armor customization and visual upgrades.

## World Design
### Planets
- **Earth**: Urban zones, rural areas, spaceports, and hidden hero bases.
- **Viltrum**: Brutal military academies, colossal arenas, and imperial cities.
- **Original Planets**: Unique civilizations with advanced defenders and strong champions.
- **Canon-inspired Worlds**: Alien empires, rebellion strongholds, and devastated battlefields.

### Open-World Features
- Multiple explorable planets connected via ship or warp gate.
- Planet hubs with NPCs, shops, mission boards, and drag-and-drop squadmates.
- Random events: invasions, rescues, rebellions, and cosmic anomalies.
- NPC interactions influence reputation and branch the story.
- Moral choices that can shift alignment between hero and conqueror.

## Story Paths
### Viltrumite Path
- Start with training on Viltrum, learning brutal combat and conquest tactics.
- Complete planetary conquest missions: defeat champions, destroy resistance, and prepare worlds for rule.
- Lead invasions, clash with rival Viltrumites, and decide whether to embrace imperial loyalty or betrayal.

### Earth Hero Path
- Start on Earth discovering powers and learning to help civilians.
- Join a hero team or become a solo defender.
- Fight villains, repel alien incursions, and eventually battle Viltrumite invaders.
- Operate from a spaceship hub with allies and launch missions across space.

## Extra 3D Elements
- Fully realized 3D environments with atmospheric shaders, volumetric clouds, and planetary scale.
- Cinematic boss arenas that emphasize verticality and destructible landmarks.
- AI opponents that use 3D tactics: flanking, altitude control, and cover usage.
- Dynamic camera that highlights action, close-ups, and spatial awareness.

## Endings and Replayability
- Branching narrative with different endings based on alignment, power level, and decisions.
- Replay value through alternate origin paths and changing reputation outcomes.
- Hybrid alignment possibilities for players who blend heroism and conquest.

## Implementation Notes
- Use a 3D engine that supports large open worlds, flight physics, and dynamic destruction.
- Design each planet with distinct visual themes and traversal challenges.
- Prioritize responsive controls for flying and melee combat.
- Balance mission variety to reward both story progress and open-world exploration.
